We’re happy to announce that Bolty mobile apps are now available on both the Play Store and the App Store. 

[Download Bolty app from Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.bolty)

[Download Bolty app from App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/bolty/id6756659984)

This launch comes after two years of building and operating Bolty on the ground. These mobile apps are a direct reflection of our internal processes and the way we interact with our customers every day.

While WhatsApp remains our primary communication channel and will continue to be so, the Bolty app is designed to give you a more structured, transparent, and reliable experience with us.

As of January 15, 2026, the apps are in public beta. This means you may encounter a few bugs or unexpected behaviors. We’re actively monitoring usage, collecting feedback, and pushing improvements regularly.

We sincerely thank all our customers for trusting Bolty and for playing a crucial role in shaping it. Your feedback and support help us move closer to our goal of building the best car makeover company.
