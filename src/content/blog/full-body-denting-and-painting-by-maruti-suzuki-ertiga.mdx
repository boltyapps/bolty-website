### Maruti Suzuki Ertiga – A Decade of Memories, Reimagined

After 10 years of love, memories, and countless journeys, our beloved customer Kiran entrusted his cherished Ertiga to Bolty for the very first time.

Our team transformed the vehicle with a premium Jeep Pearl White finish and upgraded the front-end from Type 1 to Type 2. The result was a stunning makeover that brought new life to a car filled with years of memories.

Every restoration at Bolty undergoes rigorous quality checks before delivery—because when customers trust us with something they cherish, excellence is the only standard.
